About Sweeds

Revenue Leadership You Can Trust

Dynamic revenue leader with 18+ years of hands-on experience in the GCC healthcare sector, focused on diagnostics and business model turnaround. I help struggling companies regain direction, rebuild systems, and return to profitable growth through clear strategy and strong execution.

  • Restructuring underperforming healthcare and diagnostics businesses.

  • Leading Go-To-Market execution across Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

  • Driving up to 60% YoY revenue growth in competitive markets.

  • Aligning strategy with execution through training and coaching.

  • Building sustainable, profit-focused business models.

Professional Experience

  • Beta Medical Laboratories – CEO (Current), SA

    Leading a full turnaround journey: restructuring, rebranding, and team rebuilding. Focused on creating a performance-driven culture and enabling sustainable B2C growth.

  • SWEED’S Consulting – Founder & CEO, USA

    Advising SMEs on business transformation, team development, and go-to-market strategies. Delivered practical training to leaders and frontline teams.

  • TIBYANA Healthcare – Head of Business Development, SA

    Drove partnerships and mentored business units to align strategy with execution.

  • Al Borg Diagnostics ME – Regional BD Director, SA

    Managed multi-region sales teams and led consistent revenue growth through coaching and structured performance management.
